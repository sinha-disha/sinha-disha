My research sits at the meeting point of climate hazards and social inequality, a field often called **Human-Climate Interactions**. Growing up in Kolkata, I watched each summer run hotter and each monsoon flood reach further, while the neighborhoods with the least political voice absorbed the worst of it. Today I use maps and spatial data to ask the same question in other places: who ends up in harm's way, and why?

- 🌊 **Now:** mapping wetland and land loss across coastal Louisiana as a GIS analyst at SPAARC
- 🏙️ **Master's research:** charting flood risk across Chicago's zip codes and its overlap with income, race and immigrant origin, with a focus on BIPOC (Black, Indigenous, and People of Color) communities
- 🌱 **Where it started:** field-testing whether native plantings help Chicago's parks absorb heavy rain, at the Field Museum
- 🎓 **Teaching:** led GIS labs and fieldwork for undergraduates at Indiana State University
- 🧭 **Interests:** Human-Climate Interactions · urban flood risk · environmental and climate justice · urban political geography · coastal wetland loss · GIS and remote sensing
