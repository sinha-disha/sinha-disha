I'm a geographer who studies how land, water and people shape one another. I grew up in Kolkata, one of the least climate-resilient cities in the world, and my work asks who carries the burden when a city floods, and how decisions made for the convenience of people reshape entire landscapes.

- 🌊 **Now:** building geospatial databases, spatial analyses and maps on coastal land loss in Louisiana at SPAARC
- 🏙️ **Before:** mapping how flood risk in Chicago relates to income, race and immigrant origin (MA thesis, Indiana State University)
- 🌱 **First project:** measuring how restored prairie and woodland absorb stormwater at the Field Museum, Chicago
- 🧭 **Interests:** urban stormwater flooding · environmental justice · flood risk & social vulnerability · coastal wetland loss · GIS & remote sensing

### 🛠️ Tools

ArcGIS Pro · ArcGIS Online · Survey123 · QGIS · ERDAS Imagine · Agisoft Metashape · R · Python
